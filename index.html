for several years, i have been compiling a curated list of indie/alternative music from every country/territory in the world, with an emphasis on rock subcultures and a de-emphasis on strictly anglophone countries. so far i'm up to 79!

in the making of this playlist, i've gotten to know and appreciate indie and alternative artists from music cultures around the world that i would've never been able to encounter otherwise. some highlights for me are singapore, kenya, the faroe islands, and peru!

Hey there! This interactive map displays every country I've collected so far in my playlist, with depth of shade relating to number of songs!

For more details, check out the original dataset in Google Sheets.
